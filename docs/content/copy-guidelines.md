# Copy Guidelines (Persian)

## Voice

Calm, warm, confident, plain. The reader is a worried adult on a phone. Short
sentences. Address the reader as «شما». Explain before you sell. Every page
answers: what is it, is it for me, does it hurt, how long, how much, what
happens next, how do I book.

Banned words and claims: «بهترین», «بی‌نظیر», «تضمینی», «۱۰۰٪ بدون درد»,
«ارزان‌ترین», «معجزه», any comparison that belittles other dentists, any
promise of a result. Replace with specific, checkable facts: materials,
steps, number of sessions, aftercare, who performs it.

Medical accuracy: every clinical statement is correct per current guidelines
(ADA, AAPD, AAP, Cochrane, NHS, peer-reviewed sources). Each service page and
article lists its sources and a reviewer. Treatment choice is always «با
معاینه و تشخیص دندانپزشک». Risks and aftercare are stated plainly.

## Microcopy

- One label per intent across the whole site: «رزرو نوبت», «تماس با کلینیک»,
  «پیام در واتساپ», «مسیریابی». Never two labels for the same action.
- Buttons start with a verb. Max three words.
- Forms: label above the field, example in help text, never a placeholder as
  the label. Errors say what happened and what to do: «شمارهٔ موبایل باید با
  ۰۹ شروع شود و ۱۱ رقم باشد.»
- Empty states tell the next step. Loading states name what is loading.
- Numbers: Persian digits, thousands separator «٬», amounts followed by
  «تومان», and in words where the amount matters: «۲۰۰ میلیون تومان».
- Dates: Jalali in Persian, with the weekday where helpful.

## Persian typography

- ZWNJ (U+200C) in prefixes and suffixes: می‌شود، نمی‌کند، کلینیک‌ها، درمان‌های.
- Persian ی (U+06CC) and ک (U+06A9), never Arabic ي and ك.
- Punctuation: «،» «؛» «؟» «:» and «گیومه» for quotes. No em dashes. Use
  parentheses, a colon or a new sentence instead.
- A space before an opening parenthesis and after a closing one; no space
  before «،» or «.».
- Latin words, phone numbers, URLs and codes sit in LTR islands.

## Legal lines

- Footer: clinic name, license number, technical supervisor, address, phone,
  hours, privacy link.
- Before/after pages: «تصاویر با رضایت کتبی بیماران منتشر شده‌اند. نتیجهٔ درمان
  در هر فرد متفاوت است.»
- Financing: name the partner, the maximum amount, who applies, the surcharge
  compared with cash, and «جزئیات نهایی را پذیرش اعلام می‌کند.»
- Emergency: «در صورت امکان همان روز نوبت می‌دهیم. اگر تورم تنفس یا بلع را سخت
  کرده است، با اورژانس ۱۱۵ تماس بگیرید.»
