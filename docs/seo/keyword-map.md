# Keyword Map

One row per indexable URL in `docs/ia.md`. Search volumes are **unknown**:
no keyword tool was reachable from this environment and nothing is
estimated. Validate in phase 6 with Search Console data after launch or a
keyword tool the client can access. Revised after the independent review to
remove cannibalization.

Conventions:

- **One owner per term.** Service pages own the service name, price, steps,
  side effects and area terms. Articles own comparison terms («X یا Y»,
  «تفاوت ...») and specific aftercare terms. The pricing page owns insurer
  and price-overview terms. Brand terms belong to the home page.
- Title pattern: `{topic} | کلینیک دندانپزشکی سفید`, topic under about 35
  characters. Exception: dentist pages use `| کلینیک سفید` because names and
  specialties are long. Phase 6's admin validator enforces length.
- H1 is the visible headline and carries the primary term. Service H1, title
  and nav label are stored in `docs/content/services.yaml` (`h1`,
  `seo_title`, `nav_label`); this table mirrors them.
- Intent codes: **N** navigational, **L** local, **C** commercial, **I**
  informational, **T** transactional.
- "(verify)" marks a term that implies a capability the client has not
  confirmed. "(question only)" marks a term allowed only inside a question
  heading, never as a claim («آیا کشیدن دندان درد دارد؟»).
- «متخصص» appears in copy only for a dentist with a confirmed board
  specialty. «بهترین» is never targeted.

Observation (2026-10-11, web search from outside Iran, not representative of
google.com in Tehran): local queries for خانی‌آباد returned doctor
directories (nobat.ir, doctoreto.com) and Balad listings, and no clinic
website. The brand name returned nothing. Directories are the real local
competitors in the results; a complete profile on each is a cheap citation
and a second booking channel.
