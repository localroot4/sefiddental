# Brand assets

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `logo-horizontal.png` | Original lockup from the client (1280x564) |
| `logo-horizontal.svg` | Traced vector of the full lockup: wordmark, diamonds, mark, "DENTAL . CLINIC" |
| `logo-lockup-no-latin.svg` | Wordmark, diamonds and mark without the Latin line (header use) |
| `logo-mark.svg` | The teal mark alone (favicon, loader, bullets, 3D reference) |
| `logo-wordmark.svg` | Navy wordmark «سفید» with its bar and the four diamonds |
| `logo-latin.svg` | "DENTAL . CLINIC" letterforms |
| `palette.pdf`, `palette-preview.png` | Brand palette from the client |

Meaning (client, 2026-10-12): the teal mark is toothpaste squeezed onto a
toothbrush head; the long bar of the wordmark is the brush handle. The
signage reads «کلینیک دندانپزشکی سفید» with this logo.

The SVGs are traces of the PNG (potrace at 3x, two color layers), verified
against the original at 1280 px and at 16, 32 and 64 px for the mark. Fill
colors are the brand tokens `#0A243F` and `#00827A`; swap them for the dark
theme (`#FFFFFF` and `#2BB5AA`). If the client later supplies the original
vector files, replace these.
