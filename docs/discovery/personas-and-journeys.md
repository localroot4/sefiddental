# Personas and Journeys

Five personas from `docs/brief.md` section 3, made concrete. They steer copy,
imagery, CTA placement and page order. They are never shown as segments on
the site. Ages and behaviors are design assumptions, not research data;
validate them with the receptionist after launch (what do callers ask first?).

## P1. Cosmetic seeker, «نگار»

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Profile | 22-45, works or studies, phone-first, follows clinics on Instagram |
| Goal | Whiter, even front teeth that look natural; often before an event |
| Fears | A fake "piano key" result; damage to healthy teeth; hidden costs; regret |
| Triggers | A friend's result, an Instagram reel, an upcoming wedding or photo |
| Device and moment | Phone, evening, browsing between other apps; compares 3-5 clinics |
| Entry queries | قیمت لمینت دندان، کامپوزیت یا لمینت، کامپوزیت دندان جنوب تهران |
| Decision path | Instagram or Google > service page > before/after > price range > dentist > WhatsApp question > booking |
| Objections and answers | "Will it look fake?" > before/after with consent and the CIP color-match story. "Does it damage my teeth?" > honest section on tooth preparation per option. "How much?" > from-price or how pricing works plus financing. "Who does it?" > dentist card with council number |
| Primary CTA | «رزرو نوبت» with the service prefilled; WhatsApp as the low-commitment step |

## P2. Restorative patient, «آقای رضایی»

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Profile | 35-65, lives or works in south or central Tehran, often referred by family |
| Goal | Replace a missing tooth or fix a broken one so he can eat normally |
| Fears | Pain, surgery, cost, being sold treatment he does not need |
| Triggers | Pain, a broken crown, a dentist's earlier recommendation |
| Device and moment | Phone, daytime; may ask a family member to look; calls rather than types |
| Entry queries | ایمپلنت دندان جنوب تهران، قیمت روکش دندان، دندانپزشکی خانی‌آباد، دندانپزشکی طرف قرارداد بیمه ایران |
| Decision path | Google or referral > service page > insurers and financing > address and parking > phone call |
| Objections and answers | "Is it painful?" > step list with anesthesia and aftercare. "Will insurance pay?" > named insurers. "Can I pay over time?" > financing with the transparent surcharge. "Is it close?" > map, directions, access notes |
| Primary CTA | «تماس با کلینیک» (tap to call) during hours; booking form after hours |

## P3. Anxious patient, «سارا»

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Profile | Any age; has postponed treatment for months or years |
| Goal | Get treated without feeling judged, rushed or hurt |
| Fears | Pain, needles, the sound of the drill, losing control, being scolded |
| Triggers | Pain that no longer goes away; a partner who insists |
| Device and moment | Phone, late at night; reads a lot before acting; may never call |
| Entry queries | ترس از دندانپزشکی، دندانپزشکی بدون درد، درد بعد از عصب‌کشی |
| Decision path | Article or guide > first-visit page > photos of the space and team > WhatsApp question > booking with a note |
| Objections and answers | "Will they judge me?" > first-visit page in plain words. "Can I stop if it hurts?" > stop-signal promise in the guide (confirm with the clinic). "What does the place look like?" > real photos and the tour video |
| Primary CTA | «پیام در واتساپ» first, then «رزرو نوبت» with the optional note open |

## P4. Parent, «مریم»

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Profile | 30-45, booking for a child aged 3-12 |
| Goal | A kind dentist and a calm first experience for the child |
| Fears | The child's fear becoming lifelong; unnecessary treatment |
| Triggers | A cavity seen at home, school checkup, toothache |
| Device and moment | Phone, short sessions; needs hours and parking; may book for two children |
| Entry queries | دندانپزشکی کودکان جنوب تهران، فیشور سیلانت، دندان شیری کودک |
| Decision path | Google > pediatric page > dentist profile > hours and access > booking |
| Objections and answers | "Is the dentist good with children?" > pediatric dentist profile. "How do I prepare my child?" > short guide on the pediatric page |
| Primary CTA | «رزرو نوبت» with «دندانپزشکی کودکان» prefilled |

## P5. Emergency visitor, «حمید»

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Profile | Any age, in real pain now; one-thumb use, low patience |
| Goal | Be seen today |
| Fears | Nobody answers; being told "next week" |
| Triggers | Severe pain, swelling, a broken tooth, bleeding |
| Device and moment | Phone, any time of day; may be outside opening hours |
| Entry queries | دندانپزشکی اورژانسی جنوب تهران، دندانپزشکی شبانه، درد شدید دندان |
| Decision path | Google or maps > phone button in the first viewport > call; outside hours > emergency page > request |
| Objections and answers | "Will they see me today?" > honest «در صورت امکان همان روز». "Is this dangerous?" > the safety line about ۱۱۵ |
| Primary CTA | «تماس با کلینیک» during hours; the emergency request form outside hours |

## Journey map (all personas)

```
Discover            Evaluate                   Decide                 Act                 After
Google / Maps   >   Service page           >   Dentist, price,    >   Call, WhatsApp, >   Thank-you page,
Instagram           Before/after               insurers, financing    booking form        callback, saved
Referral link       First-visit guide          Address, hours                             contact, review ask
```

Design consequences:

1. Every page offers the three actions (call, WhatsApp, booking) within one
   thumb reach on phones: the sticky bar.
2. Every service page answers the same seven questions in the same order:
   what it is, is it for me, does it hurt, how long, how much, who does it,
   what happens next.
3. Proof sits next to the claim: a before/after case next to the cosmetic
   promise, a council number next to the dentist, an insurer list next to
   the price talk.
4. Outside opening hours, call buttons stay but the copy says when the call
   will be answered, and the booking form becomes the primary action.
